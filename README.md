## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and stock records for Wanjiku, a vegetable stall owner operating at Gikomba Market. The analysis examines overall sales performance, weekly sales trends, daily sales classifications, weekday versus weekend performance, stock levels and gross profit margins. The findings are intended to help Wanjiku make informed decisions about sales improvement, inventory management and product profitability.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Overall sales:** Total sales over the 14-day period were KES 46,450.00, with an average daily sale of KES 3,317.86.
2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900.00, which was 82.50% higher than average weekday sales of KES 2,685.00.
3. **Stock and profitability:** Tomatoes and Sukuma required restocking. Avocado recorded the highest gross profit margin at 42.86%.
